Animoko

Privacy Policy

In short

Animoko collects no data. It makes no network connections, has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and contains no third-party SDKs that do. Nothing a child makes in Animoko ever leaves the computer unless the child or a parent shares a saved file themselves.

What stays on your computer

  • Animations you save (.animoko files), and films and pictures you export — saved only where you choose.
  • An automatic copy of the current animation, your own characters and simple settings (language, whether the welcome tour was shown), in the app's own folder.
  • Voice recordings, if you record them: they are stored inside your animation file.

Deleting Animoko and its files removes all of it. We never receive any of it.

Microphone

Animoko asks for the microphone only when you choose to record a voice for a character. Sound is recorded and processed only on your computer, never sent anywhere. You can refuse or withdraw access at any time in your system's privacy settings.

Children's privacy

Animoko is made for children (ages 9 and up) and their parents. Because it collects no personal information at all, it complies with children's privacy laws such as COPPA in the United States and the GDPR in the European Union by design.

Purchases

Animoko is sold by the Mac App Store and the Microsoft Store. Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Microsoft under their own privacy policies; we receive no payment or personal details.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date below. Animoko itself does not collect data, so changes are unlikely.
