Frequently asked questions

Is there a subscription, ads or in-app purchases?
No. You buy Animoko once, and everything is included — including future updates.

Does Animoko need the internet or an account?
No. It works fully offline and never asks for a name, email or login.

Where are my animations saved?
Wherever you choose when you click Save (files ending in .animoko). Animoko also keeps an automatic copy of the animation you are working on, so nothing is lost if the computer switches off.

Why does Animoko ask for the microphone?
Only so a child can record their own voice for a character, which then moves its mouth to the words. The recording stays in the animation file on your computer. You can say no — everything else still works.

The MP4 film won't save on Windows.
Windows “N” editions come without video codecs. Install the free Media Feature Pack from Settings > Apps > Optional features, or save the film as a GIF or as pictures.

Can I use Animoko on several computers?
Yes, on every computer signed in with the same Apple Account or Microsoft account. With Apple Family Sharing, your family can use it too.

Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by the store. Mac: reportaproblem.apple.com. Windows: your Microsoft account order history.
