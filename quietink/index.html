QuietInk

Private journal with AI-powered pattern detection. 100% offline.

Privacy Policy

QuietInk does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All journal entries and AI analysis results are stored exclusively on your device.

If you enable the optional iCloud Sync feature, your data is encrypted and synced through your personal Apple iCloud account. QuietInk does not have access to your iCloud data.

QuietInk does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking frameworks. No third-party SDKs are included in the application.

What data stays on your device

  • Journal entries (text, mood, timestamps)
  • AI analysis results (topics, sentiment, emotion words, detected people and places)
  • Discovered patterns and weekly summaries
  • Time capsules
  • App preferences (reminder time, iCloud toggle)

All AI analysis is performed locally using Apple's NaturalLanguage framework. No data is sent to any server, API, or cloud service (except optional iCloud backup through your Apple account).

Data deletion

You can delete individual entries at any time by swiping on them in the journal. You can also delete all data at once from Settings > Data > Delete all data.

If iCloud Sync is enabled, deleting data on one device will sync the deletion across all your devices.

Children's privacy

QuietInk is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update this page. The app itself does not collect data, so changes are unlikely.

Frequently asked questions

Is my data really private?
Yes. QuietInk makes zero network requests. All AI analysis uses Apple's on-device NaturalLanguage framework. Your journal never leaves your phone.

What does Premium include?
Premium is a one-time purchase ($4.99) that unlocks: unlimited daily entries, Discoveries (People Map, Time Heatmap, Mood Cycles, Topic Drift, Vocabulary), Weekly Summaries, Time Capsules, and Export to JSON & PDF.

Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Open QuietInk > Settings > Premium > tap "Unlock Premium" > then tap "Restore purchases" at the bottom of the paywall screen.
