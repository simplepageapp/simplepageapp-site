Deckwise

AI flashcards that help you ace your exams. 100% offline.

Privacy Policy

Deckwise does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All flashcards, decks, study progress, and AI-generated content are stored exclusively on your device.

If you enable the optional iCloud Sync feature, your data is encrypted and synced through your personal Apple iCloud account. Deckwise does not have access to your iCloud data.

Deckwise does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking frameworks. No third-party SDKs are included in the application.

What data stays on your device

  • Flashcard decks and cards (questions, answers, source text)
  • Study progress (spaced repetition schedule, ease factors, review history)
  • AI-generated content (flashcard pairs generated from your text)
  • App preferences (iCloud toggle)

All AI processing is performed locally using Apple's NaturalLanguage framework and, on supported devices, Apple's on-device Foundation Models. No text you paste or type is ever sent to any server.

Data deletion

You can delete individual cards or entire decks at any time. You can also delete all data at once from Settings > Data > Delete all data.

If iCloud Sync is enabled, deleting data on one device will sync the deletion across all your devices.

Children's privacy

Deckwise is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update this page. The app itself does not collect data, so changes are unlikely.

Frequently asked questions

Is my data really private?
Yes. Deckwise makes zero network requests. All AI flashcard generation uses Apple's on-device NLP. Your study material never leaves your phone.

What does Premium include?
Premium is a one-time purchase ($4.99) that unlocks: unlimited decks and cards, AI-powered flashcard generation, export, and iCloud sync.

How does the AI generate flashcards?
Deckwise uses Apple's NaturalLanguage framework to extract key concepts from your text and generate question-answer pairs. On newer devices with Apple Intelligence, it uses the on-device Foundation Models for higher quality results. Everything happens locally.

What is spaced repetition?
Deckwise uses the SM-2 algorithm to schedule your reviews. Cards you find easy are shown less often; cards you struggle with appear more frequently. This optimizes your learning.

Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Open Deckwise > Settings > Premium > tap "Unlock Premium" > then tap "Restore purchases."
