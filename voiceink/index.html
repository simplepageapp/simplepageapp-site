Whispr

Private voice notes that understand you. Record, transcribe, organize — 100% offline.

Privacy Policy

Whispr does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All voice recordings, transcriptions, and AI analysis results are stored exclusively on your device.

Voice recordings are saved as local audio files and never uploaded anywhere. On-device speech recognition is used for transcription — no audio is sent to any server.

If you enable the optional iCloud Sync feature, only transcription text and metadata are synced through your personal Apple iCloud account. Audio files always remain on your device. Whispr does not have access to your iCloud data.

Whispr does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking frameworks. No third-party SDKs are included in the application.

What data stays on your device

  • Voice recordings (m4a audio files)
  • Transcriptions (on-device speech recognition)
  • AI analysis results (summaries, action items, key points, topics, detected people)
  • App preferences (audio quality, iCloud toggle)

All AI analysis is performed locally using Apple's NaturalLanguage framework and, on supported devices, Apple's on-device Foundation Models. No data is sent to any server, API, or cloud service.

Microphone usage

Whispr requires microphone access to record voice memos. The microphone is only active while you are actively recording. Audio is saved locally and never transmitted.

Speech recognition

Whispr uses Apple's on-device speech recognition (SFSpeechRecognizer) with requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true. This guarantees that all transcription happens locally on your device, with zero network requests.

Data deletion

You can delete individual recordings at any time. You can also delete all data at once from Settings > Data > Delete all data. This removes all recordings, transcriptions, and analysis results permanently.

Children's privacy

Whispr is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update this page. The app itself does not collect data, so changes are unlikely.

Frequently asked questions

Is my data really private?
Yes. Whispr makes zero network requests. Speech recognition uses Apple's on-device model. Your recordings and transcriptions never leave your phone.

What does Premium include?
Premium is a one-time purchase ($4.99) that unlocks: unlimited daily recordings, full AI analysis (summaries, action items, key points), full-text search, export to PDF/TXT, and iCloud sync for transcriptions.

Does iCloud sync my audio files?
No. iCloud only syncs transcription text and metadata. Audio files always stay on the device where they were recorded.

Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Open Whispr > Settings > Premium > tap "Unlock Premium" > then tap "Restore purchases."
