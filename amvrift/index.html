AMV Rift Publisher

The publishing tool behind the AMV Rift YouTube channel.

What it does

AMV Rift Publisher is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the AMV Rift YouTube channel. It signs in to that one channel with Google and, through the YouTube Data API:

  • uploads the channel's anime music videos and Shorts, with their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails;
  • keeps the titles, descriptions and tags of the channel's own videos up to date;
  • creates the channel's playlists and adds its videos to them;
  • reads view and like counts (and YouTube Analytics reports) of the channel's own videos.

It is not offered to other users and does not act on any other channel.
